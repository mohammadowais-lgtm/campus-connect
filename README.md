# PairUp 🎓

### One real connection. Thirty minutes. Every day.

PairUp is a **campus-focused networking platform** that helps college students build meaningful real-world connections based on shared interests and availability.

Instead of endless scrolling, random messaging, or large group chats, PairUp focuses on creating **one intentional connection at a time** through short, structured meetups.

---

## 🚀 What is PairUp?

College campuses are filled with students who share similar interests, goals, and hobbies—but finding the right people to connect with can be difficult.

PairUp solves this by matching students based on:

* 🎯 Shared interests
* 🕒 Mutual availability
* 🏫 College community
* 🤝 Mutual acceptance

Once a match is found, PairUp suggests a **30-minute meetup** with a specific activity, time, and place.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎯 Interest-Based Matching

Students select their interests, allowing PairUp to discover people with similar interests and goals.

### 🕒 Availability Matching

The platform considers students' available time slots when creating matches.

### 🤝 Mutual Match

A connection becomes active only when both students accept the meetup.

### 💬 Private Chat

Chat is unlocked after mutual acceptance, reducing unwanted conversations.

### ⏱️ 30-Minute Meetups

PairUp encourages short, low-pressure interactions that can happen between classes or during free time.

### 🏫 College Verification

Students can verify their identity using their college email, keeping the community campus-focused.

### ⭐ Post-Meetup Feedback

Students can provide feedback after a meetup, helping improve future recommendations.
